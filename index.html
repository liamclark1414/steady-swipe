Add your own thoughts

Write the exact thoughts that show up for you, plus helpful replies. They join a "Mine" pack. Everything stays on this phone.

    How it works

    OCD thoughts often demand certainty, more checking, or reassurance. Helpful thoughts accept uncertainty and let the urge pass on its own.

    Each swipe is a small rep of picking the helpful response. A few minutes a day builds the habit. This app does not tell you your fears are false. It practices living with not knowing.

    This is a practice tool, not treatment. It works best alongside a therapist trained in ERP (Exposure and Response Prevention).
