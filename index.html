    How it works

    OCD thoughts ask for certainty, more checking, or reassurance. Kind thoughts make room for not knowing and let the urge pass by itself.

    Each swipe is a small rep of choosing the kind response. A few minutes a day builds the habit. Thinder won't tell you your fears are false. It practices living with not knowing.

    Thinder is a practice tool, not treatment. It works best alongside a therapist trained in ERP (Exposure and Response Prevention).
